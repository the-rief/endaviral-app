Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about EndaViral and how our SMM panel works.

What is EndaViral?
+
EndaViral is Kenya's Creator Growth Platform — three connected products in one account: social media growth services, Creator Academy (learning), and a Marketplace where brands hire creators. Everything is paid via M-Pesa.
What is Creator Academy?
+
Creator Academy is our in-platform learning hub with free and premium modules on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, AI tools, business and personal branding — written specifically for Kenyan creators, with certificates and loyalty points as you progress.
How does the Marketplace work?
+
Build a creator profile, browse campaigns posted by Kenyan businesses, and apply directly. Once a brand accepts your work, payment is released straight to your M-Pesa.
How does EndaViral work?
+
Register once and access all three pillars from one dashboard: order growth services, work through Academy modules, and apply for Marketplace campaigns — no separate sign-ups needed.
What services do you offer?
+
Followers, likes, views, comments, and other engagement-based services across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter/X, and Telegram.
How long does delivery take?
+
Most orders start within minutes and complete within 1–24 hours depending on quantity and service type.
Is EndaViral safe to use?
+
EndaViral is designed for promotional and marketing purposes. Outcomes can vary based on platform algorithms. We recommend using services responsibly.
Do you guarantee results?
+
We guarantee delivery of the service purchased as described. We do not guarantee specific outcomes such as viral growth or monetization.
Can I get a refund?
+
All orders are final once placed and payment is confirmed — refunds and cancellations are not available, so please double-check your link, quantity, and service before ordering. If an order stalls or delivers partially, our team will complete or re-queue it for you.
